## 🌟 About Express

**Express** is a modern credit card platform crafted for the ambitious generation. Built around the philosophy **"I Am Worth It"**, Express combines luxury metal craftsmanship, complete billing transparency, and smart digital onboarding to make personal credit effortless, rewarding, and stylish.

Whether you're making daily payments, converting big purchases into low-interest installments, or managing your card security on the go, Express provides an elite financial experience from start to finish.

## ✨ Key Highlights & Features

* **Precision-Crafted Metal Cards**: Heavy brushed-metal cards available in **Gold, Rose Gold, and Matte Black**, alongside sleek **Ultra Silver and Sky Blue** finishes with contactless NFC tap-to-pay.
* **Instant Digital Onboarding**: Fast, paperless application with holographic laser verification for Aadhaar and PAN cards.
* **Smart Age Eligibility**: Seamless date-of-birth validation ensuring applicants are 21 years or older, with a curated priority waitlist for younger applicants.
* **Transparent 0.99% EMI Plans**: Flexible 3, 6, and 12-month installment plans with zero hidden fees, zero compounding charges, and clear monthly schedules.
* **Dual Payment Flexibility**: Quick checkout for card issuance fees via UPI ID (with one-tap bank suffix chips) or dynamic QR code scanning.
* **Real-Time Security & CVV Eye Reveal**: Masked card numbers (`•••• •••• •••• 7163`) with an interactive eye-toggle (👁️) to view your 3-digit CVV, plus one-tap card freeze and block switches.
* **Live Account Dashboard**: Real-time tracking of approved limits, utilization percentages, zero-spent balances, and upcoming dues.
* **24/7 Virtual Concierge**: Built-in interactive assistant ready to answer questions about limits, rewards, benefits, and card delivery.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

* **Masked Card Protection**: Full card numbers are hidden; only the last 4 digits are visible on the dashboard.
* **CVV On-Demand**: The 3-digit security code is concealed behind an eye-view toggle button.
* **One-Tap Card Freeze**: Temporarily pause all online, swipe, and ATM transactions whenever you want.
* **Instant Card Blocking**: Permanently disable compromised cards and request a free replacement in seconds.
* **₹2,00,000 Zero-Liability Cover**: Protection against unauthorized or fraudulent transactions.
